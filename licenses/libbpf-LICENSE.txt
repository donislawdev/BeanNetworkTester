libbpf 1.7.0, inside libebpfdivert.so in the Linux package of Bean Network
Tester. eBPFDivert 0.0.5 builds it from its third_party/libbpf submodule,
which points at the commit of libbpf's v1.7.0 tag. Each section is a file of
libbpf at that tag, unchanged. The LGPL 2.1 text is licenses/LGPL-2.1.txt.
